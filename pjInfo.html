PROJECT OVERVIEW

Quantum fluids are remarkable materials that behave according to the laws of quantum mechanics, allowing them to flow with almost no resistance and exhibit other unusual properties with great potential for future technologies such as quantum computing, ultra-precise sensing, and efficient heat transport. This project aims to understand the most strongly interacting quantum fluids, whose behaviour cannot be explained using conventional theories. To achieve this, we will use holographic duality - a powerful mathematical framework from particle physics and string theory—together with advanced numerical simulations to reveal new physics and guide future experiments.


FUNDAMENTAL FIELD EQUATIONS

Gross-Pitaevskii equation:

$$i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t} = \left[ -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2 + V_{\text{ext}} + g|\psi|^2 \right]\psi$$


Holographic matter-gauge-gravity Lagrangian:

$$\mathcal{L} = \sqrt{-g} \left[ \frac{1}{2\kappa^2}(R - 2\Lambda) - \frac{1}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu} - (D_\mu \Psi)^* D^\mu \Psi - m^2|\Psi|^2 \right], \quad D_\mu = \nabla_\mu - iqA_\mu$$

Linear water waves beneath a thin elastic ice sheet:

$$\nabla^2\phi = 0 \quad (-h < z < 0), \quad \phi_z = 0 \quad (z = -h), \quad \eta_t = \phi_z \quad (z = 0)$$
